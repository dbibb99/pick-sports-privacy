Support for U Pick-Sports

Kaweeka Games

U Pick-Sports is a private pick'em game for friends and family. If something's not working right or you have a question, here's how to get help.

Contact Us

Email us at kaweekagames@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Please include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug — it helps us track things down faster.

Common Questions

How do I join a group?
You'll need a join code from your group's commissioner (whoever created the group). Enter it from the Groups tab to join.

I lost/forgot my join code — what do I do?
Ask your group's commissioner — they can find and share it again from their Group Info screen.

Are the tokens real money?
No. All tokens and bankrolls in U Pick-Sports are virtual, for entertainment only, and have no cash value. There's no real-money wagering, purchases, or payouts anywhere in the app.

How do I delete my account?
From the Info tab, tap Delete Account. This is permanent and removes your account entirely — see our Privacy Policy for details on what's retained.

How do I sign in?
U Pick-Sports uses Sign in with Apple exclusively — no separate password to manage.
