Privacy Policy for U Pick Sports

Last updated: August 25, 2026

U Pick Sports ("the app") is a private pick'em game for friends and family, built by Kaweeka Games ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how you can have it deleted.

Information We Collect

Account identity. You sign in exclusively with Sign in with Apple — we never collect an email address or password. Apple gives us a stable, anonymous identifier for your account. During sign-in we request your name from Apple, but we don't currently store it anywhere.

Information you provide in-app. When you join or create a group, you choose a display username and pick an avatar from a built-in set of icons — neither is required to be your real name, and there's no photo upload. Group names and join codes you create are also stored.

Gameplay data. Picks/bets you place, your token bankroll and betting history, and your group's standings. All of this uses a virtual, in-app token with no monetary value — the app involves no real-money wagering, no payment information, and no cash-outs of any kind.

Automatically collected technical data. Our server logs standard request metadata (like IP address and timestamp) for operating and securing the service. We don't use this for tracking or advertising.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We don't use your data for anything beyond running the app itself.

The app does pull public sports schedules, scores, and betting odds from third-party sports-data providers (The Odds API, TheSportsDB, collegefootballdata.com) to power gameplay — this is public sports data, not your personal information, and none of your information is sent to these providers.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from the app (Info tab → Delete Account). This immediately and permanently removes the link between your Apple ID and the app — that action can't be undone. Your picks and messages history within groups you belonged to is retained in anonymized form (your display name is replaced, your account is no longer linked to it) so that other members' group history and standings aren't broken by your departure.

Children's Privacy

U Pick Sports is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the App Store's minimum age for this app's rating. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the app requires a valid, securely issued session token.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if we add a new feature that collects different information — we'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
